# Market research: LAN physics-shooter party game

Data as of 2026-10-17. Compiled by a research agent: Steam, SteamCharts, Raijin and Metacritic were pulled directly on 2026-10-17, and everything else comes from dated reports. Items marked **(unverified)** or **(est.)** must be cited with that caveat.

> **Design update after this research.** Characters are squishy jelly cubes with mouths. They **spit pieces of their own body** as ammo, so shooting costs mass. Hits also cost the target mass and push it toward the edge. Precedents for "your ammo/propulsion is your mass" exist outside party games: Agar.io (eject mass) and Osmos (eject matter to move). Both are *to verify* before citing. So pitch the hook as "Osmos/Agar's mass economy × Smash's knockback, in a 3D LAN party shooter", not as never done before.

## 3. Gap analysis

| Opportunity | Who already does it | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Mass-as-health** (hits lower your mass, so you fly further) | Smash, and Brawlhalla-style platform fighters, get the same *effect* through a damage % counter. Literal "hits reduce mass" exists only in obscure titles: the **"Bounce" mod on ModDB** (players lose mass instead of health and eat pizza/cookies to regain weight; details only from a search snippet) [S31], and **Squarena** (2D, damage shrinks you, about 146 copies est.) [S32]. **None of the 8 comparables does it.** | **Ownable as the headline hook**, pitched as "Smash % made physical in a 3D rigidbody sim", not as never done before. Light means fast but fragile, a built-in catch-up mechanic; no comparable does that. |
| **One shooting verb remixed across mini-games** | Splatoon does this (ink shooting across Turf War, Splat Zones, Tower Control, Rainmaker, Clam Blitz) [S29], and so do Mario Kart's battle modes, but neither is a PC party scoreboard game. Pummel Party and Mario Party do the opposite, with new controls for each mini-game. | **Ownable in the PC party genre.** Pitch: "learn once, play 10 ways", with near-zero onboarding per round. |
| **LAN-first party night (one player hosts)** | On PC, only Duck Game (and Among Us) list LAN; on Switch, Splatoon 3 has official LAN Play [S28]. Fall Guys is online-only; Party Animals needs internet even for split-screen [S24]; only ≈8% of Steam party games list LAN PvP [S12]. | **Clearly underserved on PC.** But only **13% of US adult players count "offline multiplayer" as a top factor when choosing a game** (online multiplayer: 15%; price is #1 at 52%) [S7]. Treat LAN as a niche differentiator, not a mass-market driver. |
| **Eliminated players stay involved** | **Super Bomberman R's "Revenge Cart"**: knocked-out players throw bombs from the arena edge and rejoin if they score a kill [S30]. UCH and Mario Party avoid elimination altogether. Fall Guys, Gang Beasts, Party Animals and Stick Fight leave players spectating. | **Partly ownable.** For example, eliminated players become rim cannons or ghost turrets. Cite Bomberman as the precedent. |
| Running scoreboard across mini-games | Mario Party, Pummel Party's mini-game mode, UCH's points | **Not novel.** Present it as standard structure. |
| **The combination**: 3D third-person physics projectiles + mass-knockback + mini-game scoreboard + LAN host on PC | None of the 8 comparables, or the extras checked | **This combination is the defensible claim.** |

**Design risks to put in the proposal:**
1. Mass loss can snowball: hit players get lighter, get hit more, and fall further behind. Smash avoids this by resetting % on each KO. Reset or regenerate mass per mini-game, or give light players a speed bonus.
2. LAN-only play has no matchmaking or discovery. Steam Remote Play Together already covers "couch with remote friends".
3. Steam revenue is winner-take-all (M9).

## 4. Target audience

**A. Gen Z friend groups on PC, about 16–29, students in particular (core segment)**
- In the US, more than 80% of Gen Z (ages 14–29) play weekly; 89% have played with others; 88% of Gen Z adults agree games "bring different types of people together" [S7].
- Among US teen players: 89% play with others, 72% name time with others as a reason to play, and 49% play on PC (survey Sep–Oct 2023, published 2024-05-09) [S9].
- UK students (UNiDAYS, n=1,005, 2024-10-20): **46% prefer party/puzzle games** (one combined category), with an average **£48/month** gaming spend. Every respondent played, so the sample was likely gamers only [S10].
- This group demonstrably buys cheap social PC games: R.E.P.O. 19.2M players and PEAK 14.5M in 2025 [S5].

**B. Hosts of in-person game nights: dorms, student societies, LAN clubs, office or family events (Millennials 30–45 and parents)**
- 71% of US Millennials play weekly and about 76% have played with others (chart read) [S7, S8].
- 81% of parent players play with their kids, 52% at least weekly [S8].
- 55% of play time falls on Fri–Sun, and 62% of social players play with friends they know in person [S7]. That is the "one host, everyone in the room" use case.

**C. Clip and streamer-driven viewers (discovery channel, not a buyer segment by itself)**
- Fall Guys peaked at **708.8K Twitch viewers on 2020-08-28** [S34].
- The Party Animals demo drew **113K Twitch viewers** in Oct 2020 [S23].
- 90% of Gen Z online players use in-game chat or voice [S7].
- Slapstick physics knockback is clip-friendly by design.

Price signal: indie PC comparables sit at **$5–$20** (Stick Fight $4.99, UCH and Pummel $14.99, Gang Beasts and Party Animals $19.99), and price is the #1 purchase factor for US adult players at 52% [S7].

## 5. Unverified items (do not overstate)
- **Raijin sales and revenue are model estimates** and get revised heavily (Gang Beasts went from 1.3M to 2.5M between July and Oct 2026). Raijin's own "all-time peak" figures cover only its tracking window, so SteamCharts or SteamDB peaks are used instead.
- **Party Animals peak:** SteamDB 104,174 (via press) vs SteamCharts 57,806. Use the SteamDB figure with attribution.
- **Fall Guys Steam figures are understated:** new PC players have only been able to get it on Epic since June 2022.
- **No official cumulative sales** found for Party Animals, Gang Beasts, Pummel Party or UCH.
- **No 2024+ official revenue** for Fall Guys or Stumble Guys. Stumble Guys figures date from Jan 2023.
- **Smash Ultimate wired LAN mode:** not confirmed by any official source (Local Wireless is confirmed). Latest Splatoon 3 total is from Mar 2024.
- **Bounce mod:** ModDB blocked fetching, so the mechanic description comes from a search snippet only, and the host game is unconfirmed.
- **ESA 2026 generation figures** (91/89/76/51/35%) were read from PDF chart positions. The headline 70% / 62% / 46% figures are printed text.
- **Implied Nintendo revenue** (units × $59.99) is a calculation and an upper bound, not reported revenue.
- **The $12.4B "party games market"** figure is unreliable: it includes board games.
