# Knockback Arena v2 asset kit

Everything Knockback Arena needs to look, sound and feel finished, generated by code and verified in Unity 6000.3.9f1 / URP 17.3:
- the CubeCritter character (below);
- the candy-goo spit;
- the ice arena and sky;
- props (leader crown, jump spring);
- VFX, UI/HUD, SFX and music;
- a playable local round that shows it all.

Research notes behind the choices are in `research/`:
- `design_brief.md` and `anim_spec.md` (character);
- `ice_brief.md` (arena);
- `vfx_brief.md` (spit and VFX);
- `ui_audio_brief.md` (HUD and SFX).

## Try it

Open `Assets/Ezel/KnockbackArena/Scenes/KnockbackArena_Ice.unity` and press Play.

1. **Lobby.** Press Enter or click START. HOST and JOIN are view-only: networking hooks them up.
2. **3-2-1-GO!** Then play:
   - WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to spit.
   - **Shift** to dash (3 charges).
   - **Space** to jump, which needs a spring pickup.
3. **Knockouts.** Spit the three bots off the ice. Every hit lowers the target's mass: the cards and rings show it, and later hits push further.
4. **Results.** When one critter is left, the podium rises with the crown, confetti and fanfare.
   - Points are 4/3/2/1, plus 1 comeback point for whoever is last overall.
   - **R** plays again (the leader now wears the crown in play). **Esc** goes back to the lobby.

Review renders from Unity are in `renders/v2/unity/`: 12 stills plus `gameplay_unity.mp4`. The sounds are in `renders/v2/sfx_reel.mp3`.

## Rebuild

| Step | Command (from `blender/v2/`) |
|---|---|
| Character + spit blob | `Blender -b --factory-startup --python make_all.py` |
| Arena, backdrop, podium, crown, spring | `Blender -b --factory-startup --python make_world.py` (also saves `IceArena.blend`) |
| Sprites, UI sprites | `PY sprites.py` |
| Ice textures, sky panorama | `PY env_textures.py` |
| SFX (27 files) | `PY sfx.py` (self-checks loudness, clicks, DC) |
| Music loops | `PY music_loop.py <in.wav> <out.wav>`: cuts a Higgsfield Seed Audio clip to whole bars and folds the ring-out over the loop point |
| Portraits | `Blender -b CubeCritter_v2.blend --python render_previews.py -- portraits` |
| Unity | Menu **Knockback Arena → Rebuild Everything**: materials, VFX, prefabs, UI, sound bank, both scenes |

`PY` = `/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/Resources/5.2/python/bin/python3.13`. It is Blender's bundled Python, which has numpy; no pip is needed.

**Unity needs TMP Essential Resources** in `Assets/TextMesh Pro`. They are committed alongside. If they are missing: Window → TextMeshPro → Import TMP Essential Resources.

## What was built (Unity paths under `Assets/Ezel/KnockbackArena/`)

### Spit: "what she throws"

`SpitBlob.prefab` is a glossy candy-goo ball in the shooter's colour. Shape and material:
- Radius 0.17 m (16 px at 20 m); the hitbox is 0.15.
- Shader `KA/Goo`: the core lifts toward white (lemon for Chartreuse), with a saturated rim, 25 % self-emission and a 70 % brightness floor, so it never reads as snot. A cartoon glint is locked in view space.
- Always convex: no strands or drips.

Lifecycle (`SpitBlob.cs`, timings from the VFX brief):
1. Squirts out: 0.5 → 1.15 → 1.0 scale in 120 ms.
2. Stretches into an egg with speed and jiggles at 7 Hz.
3. Trails a droplet chain, sparkles and a soft ribbon.
4. On a critter: hit-stop, then it flattens, sinks in and shrinks over 180 ms, and the target jiggles and flashes white.
5. On the ice: it splats, throws droplets, a white star and ice chips, and leaves a 3 s decal.
6. On timeout: it pops into sparkles.

Fix found on the way: Unity imports this blend-shape-only mesh with a plain MeshRenderer, which never applied Stretch/Squash, even in v2.0. The prefab now uses a SkinnedMeshRenderer, and a test guards it.

### Ice arena and world

Arena prefabs (`Prefabs/Arena/`):
- **`Arena_Ice_16`:** the default 16 × 16 m floe.
  - It is 4×4 kit tiles with 4 m rounded corners, plus the 11 m `Island_Underside` (tiered, off-centre tip).
  - There is **one floor collider**, `PM_Ice` (friction 0.3). Per-tile colliders snag sliding boxes at the seams.
- **Kit (`Prefabs/Arena/Kit/`):** `Ice_Top_4x4`, `Ice_Edge_4_A/B`, `Ice_Corner`, plus floes and crystal clusters.
  - The rim noise fades to one canonical profile at tile ends, so any tile joins any other.
  - Custom layouts need their own floor collider.
- **`Arena_Backdrop`:** 3 floes and 3 crystal clusters, 38–80 m out, fogged, no shadows.
- **`Podium_Ice`:** 4 octagonal ice columns that rise out of the floor for the results.

Shader `KA/Ice` (compile-checked URP HLSL, Metal):
- Vertex colours carry data: height, snow, AO/facet shade, sparkle.
- Mapping is world-space XZ, so no UVs are needed.
- It draws the dark tile-seam grooves, fresnel rim, an inner parallax crack layer, view-dependent glints and an ice-only blue shadow tint.
- **White means drop:** only the edge lip is white, a flush 8 mm snow nose.

Sky and atmosphere:
- `T_Sky_Panorama`: 4096×2048, imported as a cubemap, on `M_Sky`.
- Deep blue, with a horizon haze that equals the fog colour #4A92E2, puffy clouds and a thin cloud bank over the deep-blue drop.
- Lighting is realtime only: sun 1.2 at 58°, gradient ambient, linear fog 30–160 m, Bloom threshold 1.1 (keeps Chartreuse off the ice).
- Budget: about 20 k triangles for the whole environment (the brief's cap is 150 k).

### Props

- **`Props/Crown`:** candy-gold plastic crown with a ruby. It sits around the goo curl at a jaunty tilt and rides the `Top` bone.
  - `LeaderCrown.Attach(critter)` adds it with a drop-on pop.
  - Used for the session leader (pitch comeback rule) and on the podium.
- **`Props/Pickup_Jump`:** gold spring with pink pads and a white chevron, plus a gold ground ring.
  - It bobs, spins and breathes, with idle glints and a collect burst.
  - It gives `CritterMotor.jumpCharges`, then respawns after 8 s.

### VFX (`VFX/`, built by `Editor/VfxSetup.cs`)

The effects are white sprites tinted at runtime: `Fx.Play`, with child names `Tint*` and `Pastel*`, so one prefab serves all four players. They are:
- `VFX_SpitPop`
- `VFX_Impact`
- `VFX_TimeoutPop`
- `Tint_Trail`
- `VFX_SpeedLines`: while pushed faster than 6 m/s
- `VFX_LandPuff`
- `VFX_DashBurst`
- `VFX_FallPoof`: stars, at the out height
- `VFX_Confetti`: 3D-spinning sheet quads
- `VFX_PickupSparkles` and `VFX_PickupCollect`
- `VFX_SnowAmbient`
- `VFX_SplatDecals`: a 64-particle floor decal system, so the URP Decal feature isn't needed

Also:
- `BlobShadow`: a Mario-style contact shadow.
- `CameraShake`: trauma², local only, and it never moves the aim.
- Visual-only hit-stop in `CritterAnimator`. `Time.timeScale` is never touched, so it is safe for networking.

The sprites come from `sprites.py`: 14 particle and decal textures, plus 12 UI sprites.

### UI (`UI/`, built by `Editor/UiSetup.cs`)

- **Font:** Fredoka Bold and SemiBold (OFL, `UI/Fonts/`) as static TMP SDF assets, with ink-outline presets.
- **`HUD_KnockbackArena`:**
  - 4 player cards: portrait, name, **mass %** and a drain bar. A hit punches, shakes and floats the loss; the colour runs white → amber below 70 % → pink below 40 % (pulsing). Score and crown too.
  - Crosshair with a cooldown ring.
  - Dash pips and the jump slot.
  - Countdown 3-2-1-GO!
  - **OUT!** banner for you, a toast for the others.
  - Results rows.
- **`MassRing`:** world-space mass ring above each critter, with an ink keyline so Chartreuse and Violet read on ice and sky.
- **`Lobby_KnockbackArena`:**
  - Logo, HOST, JOIN by IP and port.
  - 4 slots with portrait, name and READY tick (never colour alone).
  - READY and START buttons.
  - It is view-only, with events for the network code.
- **Portraits:** `Portraits/Portrait_CC_<Player>_<Neutral|Happy|Hurt|Sad>.png`, rendered from the model. The cards swap to Hurt on hits and Sad when out.

### Audio (`Audio/`, bank `Audio/SFX_Bank.asset`, player `SfxPlayer`, calls `Sfx.Play("Hit", pos)`)

- **SFX:** 27 procedural files from `sfx.py`. Loudness targets are from the brief, with peaks at or below −1 dBFS:
  - Spit ×3, Hit ×3, Splat ×2, BlobPop, Whoosh, Wheee, Splash, Land, Step ×4
  - Beep, Go, KO jingle, Fanfare
  - UI click / hover / back
  - Jump boing, Pickup chime, DashReady
- **Music:** `MUS_Gameplay` (117 BPM, 8 bars, 16.4 s) and `MUS_Lobby` (121 BPM, 8 bars, 15.9 s).
  - Both come from Higgsfield Seed Audio (2 generations, 0.2 credits in total), cut to seamless loops.
- **Import settings** are applied by `KnockbackAudioImport`: PCM / ADPCM / Vorbis by group, music streamed.

### Game code touched (playground values; gameplay owns the tuning)

- **`CritterMotor`:**
  - `massLossPerHit`: hits lower mass, not size. A hit uses the pre-hit mass.
  - `Dash()` with charges and recharge.
  - `jumpCharges` (-1 = unlimited, as in the playground). A lighter cube jumps higher.
  - Events `Landed`, `Dashed`, `Jumped`, `Knocked`.
- **`CritterFx`:** speed lines, landing puff, dash burst, footsteps.
- **`Playground/KnockbackMatchDemo` and `ArenaBotAI`:** a local, single-machine round. This is not the networked flow; the real one replaces it and keeps the views.

## Known limits / next steps

- **Nobody has listened to the audio yet.**
  - The SFX are synthesized to recipes and checked by measurement, not by ear.
  - The music clips are only about 16 s (the Seed Audio length). Longer tracks would cost about 3.75 credits per minute with Sonilo; that's the user's call.
- **`KA/Ice` and `KA/Goo` are only compile-checked on Metal** (Mac). Check D3D11/Vulkan once on a Windows machine.
- **The match demo and bots are local placeholders**, with no NGO. The lobby buttons only raise events.
- **Kit tiles for custom layouts need a matching floor collider.** Only `Arena_Ice_16` ships one.
- **The default `PC_RPAsset` shadow distance and cascades are unchanged** (shared team settings). The ice brief suggests 40 m and 2 cascades.

# CubeCritter v2: Knockback Arena player character

The v2 player character is a glossy vinyl-toy cube creature. Everything is generated by code:

- one skinned mesh, with the face built as geometry islands and 11 blend shapes;
- a 12-bone Generic rig;
- 13 in-place clips at 30 fps;
- a spit projectile.

It is exported to Unity 6 / URP and wired into prefabs, an Animator and a playable test scene. v1 (`../CubeCharacter.blend`, `Assets/.../CubeCharacter.fbx`) is untouched.

## Design (what and why)

Research notes behind these choices, with sources, are in `research/`:

- `design_brief.md`: baby schema, back-view hooks, TF2 readability, the colour-blind check.
- `anim_spec.md`: key-pose tables, spit frame data, layering.
- `blender52_unity_export_reference.md`: the verified Blender 5.2 → Unity export path.

- **Shape.**
  - The body is a superellipsoid (p = 3.8), W:H:D = 1 : 0.94 : 0.92. The bottom is 5% wider than the top.
  - It is an even quad grid (20 × 20 per face), so squash, stretch and cheek puff deform smoothly.
  - Size: 1.0 m wide, about 1.2 m tall including the curl. 9,260 triangles.
- **Face sits low** (baby schema).
  - Eye centres at 46% of body height. Mouth at 29%, blush just below and outside the eyes.
  - Tall glossy oval eyes with two catchlights each.
  - Facial parts sitting low is the strongest single shape cue for cuteness in the research.
- **Face = geometry islands + blend shapes, not a texture atlas.**
  - Eyes, catchlights, mouth, tongue and blush are separate islands floating 1.5 mm above the body, in the same skinned mesh.
  - Shapes that morph, such as oval → closed line or smile → "O", are grid-to-grid blend shapes.
  - Shapes that can't morph (`> <` and the tongue) are islands collapsed inside the body and expanded by their key.
  - The result: real 3D gloss, smooth blends, and no custom Unity shader.
  - Blend shapes: `Blink`, `EyesHappy` (^^), `EyesAngry`, `EyesWide`, `EyesHurt` (> <), `MouthOpen` (D + tongue), `MouthO` ("ptoo"), `MouthSmallO` (pucker), `MouthW` (ω), `MouthFrown`, `CheekPuff`.
  - Keep each group (eyes, mouth) summed at 1 or below. `validate.py` checks this on every frame of every clip.
- **Hooks.**
  - **Front:** cheeks that puff before every spit, which opponents can read as a warning.
  - **Top:** a goo curl on a 2-bone spring. It is readable from the over-the-shoulder camera and could double as a cooldown cue.
  - **Back:** a lighter-tint emblem, one shape per player (dot, ring, stripes, chevron). The player stares at the back all match, and colour is never the only cue.
- **Palette** (colour-blind checked against the arena colours):
  - Tomato `#FF4F2E`, Teal `#00B3A4`, Chartreuse `#D4F21C`, Violet `#7B2FD0`.
  - Under deuteranopia the old orange and lime nearly merged (ΔE ≈ 7), and cyan was weak on ice.
  - To change colours, edit `PLAYERS` in `materials.py` and `Players` in `Editor/CubeCritterSetup.cs`.
- **Material: opaque gummy vinyl.**
  - Blender: Principled BSDF, Coat 1.0, Subsurface 0.18.
  - Unity: URP Complex Lit with clear coat (smoothness 0.62, coat 0.93).
  - No real transparency, because it reads badly on ice and costs sorting and overdraw.

## Rig (Generic, in place)

```
Root ─┬─ Foot.L, Foot.R           feet never inherit body squash
      └─ COG ── Body ─┬─ Top ── Curl.1 ── Curl.2      Top = jelly lag of the upper half
                      ├─ Arm.L, Arm.R
                      ├─ Muzzle   socket: projectile spawn point, at the mouth
                      └─ Face     control bone: its custom props drive the blend shapes
```

- **Bone axes.** All deforming bones share Body's rest orientation (up, roll 0), so squash never shears them at rest.
  - Local Y = up (scale Y = squash).
  - Local X = right side (rotation X+ = lean forward).
  - Local Z = forward.
- **Skinning.**
  - The body is weighted smoothly by height between Body and Top.
  - Arms and feet are rigid.
  - The curl is weighted along its length.
- **Face animation is keyed as custom properties on `Face`, with drivers feeding the shape keys.**
  - This is what makes Blender 5.2's FBX exporter bake correct per-clip `blendShape.*` curves.
  - The exporter only swaps the armature's action for each take. Direct shape-key actions end up baked identically into every clip (verified in Unity).

## Clips (30 fps, in place; code moves the character)

| Clip | Frames | Loop | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Idle | 60 | yes | Breathing squash, yaw drift. No baked blinks (they're random at runtime). |
| Run | 12 | yes | 5 steps/s. Planted-foot keys are linear: **authored at 2.6 m/s**. Set `RunRate = speed / 2.6`, clamped to 0.85–1.35. |
| Spit | 22 | no | F0 cheeks puff and eyes go ^^ (visible on press). **F4 = 133 ms: the blob leaves.** F5 lunge stretch, F7 recoil, F10–16 smug ^ω^. Re-spit allowed from F10. |
| Hit | 20 | no | F0–2 hitstop shake, > o < squash. F3–9 rebound. F11–17 wet-dog shake-off. |
| Fall | 12 | yes | Airborne panic: flailing arms, pedalling feet, "waaa!" |
| Land | 10 | no | Squash 0.76, then overshoot. |
| Jump | 10 | no | Crouch, then stretch. Ends in the airborne pose. |
| Victory → VictoryLoop | 45 / 30 | no / yes | Crouch, spin-hop 0.3 m, arms up, ^^ grin; then a hopping loop. |
| Defeat → DefeatLoop | 40 / 60 | no / yes | Wide eyes, deflate, frown, pout, sigh; then a slow sad breath. |
| Taunt | 36 | no | Puff and hop-wiggle, then a "pfft" and a smug blink. |
| Blink | 6 | no | Face-only clip (the runtime blink in `CritterAnimator` normally handles it). |

Secondary motion is baked: the Top jiggle and curl follow-through come from a damped-spring simulation of the body's acceleration (`anim.py: bake_spring`). Feet use pivot compensation, so toe-off and heel strike stay on the ground.

## Using it in game code (Unity)

- **Drop in** `Prefabs/CubeCritter_<Player>.prefab`. It contains a Rigidbody, a BoxCollider (0.92 × 0.98 × 0.86, `PM_Critter`), `CritterMotor`, `CritterSpit`, and an Animator with `CritterAnimator`.
- **Animator parameters:**
  - `Speed` (m/s), `RunRate` and `Grounded`;
  - triggers `Spit`, `Hit`, `Jump`, `Win`, `Lose`, `Taunt`.
  - Call these through `CritterAnimator` (`SetLocomotion`, `PlaySpit`, `PlayHit`, ...).
- **Layers:**
  - **Base:** locomotion blend tree, Jump/Airborne/Land, Hit, Victory, Defeat, Taunt.
  - **Action:** override layer with the upper-body + face mask, used for Spit. `CritterAnimator` raises its weight only while a spit plays, so you can spit while running.
- **Spitting:**
  - `CritterSpit.TryFire(aimDir)` turns to aim, plays Spit, and spawns `SpitBlob` at `Muzzle` 4/30 s later, on a timer rather than an animation event. The cooldown is 10/30 s.
  - The blob is physical (custom gravity 3 m/s²). It stretches with speed, splats with its `Squash` shape, and calls `CritterMotor.Knockback` on the critter it hits.
  - The target plays Hit and flashes white.
  - The numbers (speed 12, knockback 3.5, friction 0.4) are playground values. Gameplay owns mass/knockback rules.
- **Networking (NGO):** use `NetworkAnimator` for the triggers. The clips are in place, so `NetworkTransform` owns position.
- **Playground:**
  - Open `Scenes/CubeCritter_Playground.unity` and press Play.
  - Controls: click to capture the mouse, WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to spit, Space to jump, T to taunt, V to win, B to lose. Gamepad also works.
  - Three dummies wander and spit back.

## Verified

- **Blender `validate.py`:** all 13 clips pass. On every frame, the face groups stay at 1 or below, nothing goes below the ground and every loop closes.
- **Unity 6000.3.9f1 batch import** (run in a scratch copy of the project):
  - 13 clips with clean names and loop flags, plus 11 blend shapes.
  - Neutral default face. The FBX exporter writes the last take's face as the default, so the import postprocessor zeroes it.
  - Stands on y = 0, about 1.2 m tall, faces +Z.
- **PlayMode tests (`Tests/`), all passing:**
  - `ImportedClipsAndBlendShapes`.
  - `SpitAnimatesTheFace`: CheekPuff passes 50 during the charge.
  - `SpitKnocksBackAnotherCritter`: the blob spawns, the cooldown holds, the target is pushed and plays Hit.

## Known limits / next steps

- **VFX and SFX** are now covered by the asset kit above (`VFX/`, `Audio/`).
- **URP Complex Lit has no rim light.** A Shader Graph with a Fresnel rim and wrap lighting (values in the design brief) would bring the Unity look closer to the Blender renders.
- **Edit-mode previews show a neutral face** even when a clip is selected: batch edit-mode sampling doesn't apply blend shapes. Play mode is correct (tested).
- **Unity names the imported mesh child objects `CubeCritter` / `CubeCritter_Mesh`**, with a 270° X rotation from the axis conversion. That is the standard Blender→Unity result; the prefab root is identity.
